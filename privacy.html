Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Orama Media is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safe-keep information acquired during client bookings, consultations, and visits to our website.

1. Information Collection

We collect information you supply when submitting an enquiry or booking a consultation on our site, including your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, event details, and budget.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is strictly used to reply to enquiries, customize videography assignments, verify local Zimbabwean drone flight compliance permits, and process ZIMRA corporate invoices.

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard secure storage practices to protect client details and contact logs from unauthorized access. We do not sell or share client information with third parties.
